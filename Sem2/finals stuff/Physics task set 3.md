# task 1.
The lift can move up and down with acceleration of the same value. A student stands
on a spring scale in the lift. The difference in the student weight display when moving
up and down is 50 N. What is the acceleration of the lift if the weight of the student is
500 N? 

$F=m·a$
student $500N=m·9.8m/s^{2}\implies m=51kg$

force of the lift $F=\pm 25N$

student going up
$F=m·g+m·a_{lift}=500N+51kg·a$
$525N=500N+51kg·a$
$25N=51kg·a$
$a=0.49m/s^{2}$

student going down
$475N=500N-51kg·a$
$-25=-51kg·a$
$a=0.49m/s^{2}$

Ans: the lift is accelerating at $0.49m/s^{2}$

# task 2.
In a carriage moving horizontally with some acceleration, a weight with a mass of
100g is suspended on a thread, which deviates from the vertical direction by an angle
of 15°. Calculate the acceleration of the carriage and the tension force of the thread.
![[Pasted image 20260908113241.png]]

$\vec{T}=\vec{mg}+\vec{ma}$
$mg=9.8m/s^{2}·0.1kg=0.98N$ down
$ma=mg·\tan\alpha=0.263N$ to the left
$0.1kg·a=0.263N$
$a=\frac{0.263}{0.1}=2.62m/s^{2}$
$T=\sqrt{ 0.98^{2}+0.263^{2} }=1.014N$
