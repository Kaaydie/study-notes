Magnetic flux, Gauss law



Gauss law
$\Phi=\oint_{S} \vec{E} \cdot d\vec{S}=\frac{Q_{int}}{\epsilon_{0}}$
$\Phi$ - electric flux
$S$ - closed surface ( Gaussian surface)
$Q_{int}$ - total charge

$\vec{E}=\frac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_{0}} \frac{1}{r^{2}} \frac{\vec{r}}{r}$


The electric flux $\Phi$ coming through any closed surface $S$ (Gaussian surface) is equal to the total charge $Q_{int}$ enclosed by this surface divided by $\epsilon_{0}$
